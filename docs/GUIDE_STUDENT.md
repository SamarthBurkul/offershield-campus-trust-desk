# OfferShield guide for students

Got a job or internship offer that feels too good, or that asks you to pay? **Check with your placement cell before you pay or share anything.**

## Getting started

1. Open OfferShield and choose **Register as a student**.
2. Enter your name, email, a password, your year and branch, and the **college code** and **join code** your placement cell gave you.
   - Use your college email address. If your college does not give students an email address, use your personal email: your placement cell will approve your account (you will see "Waiting for your placement cell to approve your account" until then).
3. Read and accept the privacy notice and terms.

Forgot your password? Ask your placement cell to reset it. They will give you a temporary password, and you choose a new one when you sign in.

## Check before you pay

On your home page, type the UPI ID, phone number, email, website or company name from the message into **Check before you pay**:

- **Confirmed fraud** (red): your placement cell has confirmed this is a scam. Do not pay or reply.
- **Look-alike warning** (amber): the address imitates a company your placement cell verified, but it is a different address. Treat it as suspicious.
- **Verified by your placement cell** (green): your placement cell has verified this company's contact. Still never pay to apply or join.
- **Verification expired** (grey): it was verified before, but the check is out of date — ask your placement cell.
- **Not reviewed:** your placement cell has no record of it. That does **not** mean it is safe — ask them to check.

## Ask the placement cell to verify an offer

1. Choose **Ask your placement cell to verify an offer**.
2. Paste the message, add the links and contacts, say whether money was asked for or already paid, and upload screenshots (PNG, JPEG or PDF, up to 3 MB each). Crop out your own ID cards and bank details.
3. Follow the request under **My requests**. If the placement cell asks a question, reply there. You will see a notification when there is news or an outcome.

If you **already paid**, say so in the request and also report it straight away on **cybercrime.gov.in** or by calling **1930**.

## Advisories

When a scam is going around, your placement cell publishes an advisory for the batches at risk. Read it and press **I've read this**.

## Stay safe

- Genuine employers **never** charge a registration, training, kit, laptop or security-deposit fee.
- Never share an OTP, your bank details, Aadhaar or PAN with a recruiter you have not verified.
- Be suspicious of urgency ("pay within 24 hours"), Telegram/WhatsApp-only contact, and "like and earn" tasks.
- An outcome from your placement cell is their finding based on the checks they made, not a guarantee. OfferShield never certifies an offer as safe.

Questions about your data? See the **Privacy** page (link at the bottom of every page) or ask your placement cell.
